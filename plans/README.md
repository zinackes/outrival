### Direction plans (019 to 023)

- **All five are independent of each other and of 001 to 018.** They can be
  executed in any order, or interleaved with the deep-run plans.
- **025 came out of 024's autopsy.** Querying the queue to falsify 024 surfaced
  `outrival-dlq` holding 602 jobs while the `/admin` tile that exists for exactly
  that reads `25`, because the count is the length of a list capped at `DLQ_CAP`.
  Nothing alerts on it either. Small, live, and independent of everything else.
- **024 is REJECTED, and the reason is worth keeping.** It was written on a
  measurement that was true but mis-framed: 105 of 109 new competitors got a
  backfillable snapshot and only 21 produced a `backfill_runs` row. Splitting that
  by day before writing the fix showed why: 85 first captures between 2026-06-28 and
  2026-07-13 produced 2 backfill rows, while 20 first captures since 2026-07-22
  produced 37, which is the expected homepage-plus-pricing pair per competitor. The
  queue confirms it: all 37 `backfill-history` jobs over 28 days are `completed`,
  none expired or failed. The backfill works; the gap closed on 2026-07-22. The plan
  file stays as the record. Do not execute it.
- **The 27% SLI from 019 is therefore a historical average, not a live state.** It
  spans the era before the backfill ran. Post-2026-07-22 there are 2 completions,
  1 inside the 10-minute mark, which is n=2 and not a measurement. Re-measure at
  n>=10 before drawing any conclusion or acting on the error-budget freeze.
- **024 was 019's output.** 019 routed to wiring; the follow-up measurement then
  localised it: over 28 days, 109 competitors were created, 105 got a `homepage` or
  `pricing` snapshot, and only **21** ever produced a `backfill_runs` row. The
  enqueue site is unconditional with no early exit, and the handler is registered,
  so the loss sits between enqueue and execution. 024 diagnoses which pg-boss job
  state accounts for it before changing anything.
- **019 ran on 2026-07-25 and its result changes the backlog.** The 28-day window
  measured 22 completions, 16 missed, 5 never-signal: an SLI of 27% against a 70%
  target, so the SLO's **window-exhausted** condition has fired and the error-budget
  policy applies (freeze on non-reliability changes to the onboarding to
  first-signal path). The dominant miss bucket is `no_backfill_run` at 14 of 16,
  which routes to **wiring**, not coverage. The follow-up is therefore "find why the
  backfill never fires for most first scrapes", NOT the guaranteed day-0 artifact the
  SLO doc originally anticipated. None of 020 to 023 touches the frozen path.
- **019 before any onboarding or coverage work.** It answers which of three
  possible causes is losing roughly six in ten onboarded workspaces before their

  coverage, wiring, or latency is a coin flip.
- **019 and 022 both need production access** to complete (the admin readout and

- **021 touches every capability's liveness but changes no switch.** It is

## Findings considered and rejected (so they aren't re-audited)

- **Tenant isolation** — all 44 route files were walked mechanically; **zero IDOR**.
  Every non-admin handler with a path parameter re-asserts ownership. The five
  routers without `authMiddleware` are exactly the documented-public ones, and the
  two "public" ones mention it only in comments. Verified healthy, not a finding.
- **Repo-wide formatter (Biome/Prettier)** — a formatting sweep would rewrite
  ~1450 tracked files while ~200 unmerged local branches and 55 remote
  `advisor/*` branches are in flight, on a RAM-constrained box. The repo is
  consistently hand-formatted by one author and `.claude/rules/karpathy.md`
  already forbids reformatting adjacent code. `.editorconfig` is worth adding;
  the formatter is not. Not rejected on merit for a future team, rejected for now.
- **Component/E2E test layer for `apps/web`** — 396 components, zero component
  tests, no DOM library. A full suite is too much surface for the value; one smoke
  path (login → dashboard → open a signal) is the honest ceiling and should be
  decided on its own.
- **`@opentelemetry/api` override removal** — the override forces Sentry below its
  declared `^1.9.1` range. Its only cause is `@trigger.dev/sdk` pinning `1.9.0`,
  so it becomes removable for free once Trigger leaves the tree. Revisit then, not
  now.
- **Transitive moderate/low advisories** (`esbuild` ×4 versions, `protobufjs`,
  `dompurify`, `@opentelemetry/core`) — all build-time or dev-path, none reachable
  from request handling. They clear as a side effect of plan 010's bumps or not
  at all.
- **`packages/scrapers`' 40 export subpaths** — looks like manifest bloat, is
  load-bearing: each is a documented Chromium-free / AI-free leaf that keeps
  Playwright out of the API's parse graph. It is the pattern `@outrival/shared`
  should copy, not reduce.
- **`typescript` 5.9 → 7.0 and `shiki` 1 → 4** — real lag, but a compiler change
  and a three-major jump with silent visual-regression risk are their own
  decisions, not dependency hygiene.

The direction audit was run against production rather than intuition. The numbers
below are the evidence the five plans rest on; re-measure before treating any of
them as current.

Product analytics, 90 days to 2026-07-25: 24 `user_signed_up`, 26
`onboarding_completed`, **11 `first_real_signal`**, 216 `signal_generated`. Weekly
active people on `/dashboard` over the last four weeks: 10, 8, 1, 3. Last
`user_logged_in` event: 2026-07-06.

Monetization, 180 days: `paywall_shown` fired for exactly two reasons,
`plan_locked_source` (9 events, 6 people) and `plan_limit_competitors` (6 events,
3 people). Never a capability lock, never a quota. One `paywall_cta_clicked` in the
whole period.

Repository shape over the same window: 50 commits in 30 days, **43 of them
`feat(web)` or `fix(web)`**. Indexable surface: 27 static routes of which 15 are
legal pages, plus 3 blog posts.

The one-line read: this is not a product short of capabilities. It is a
feature-rich product with almost no users, whose core promise (a signal on day 0)
measurably fails for roughly six onboarded workspaces in ten, and whose habit
surface (the inbox) contains no link back into the app.

## Direction findings considered and rejected (so they aren't re-audited)

- **Multi-user / RBAC (roadmap Phase 10)** and **the public read API (Phase 11)**.
  Both are correctly hidden today and the reasoning is already written in
  `docs/paid-feature-delivery.md`, including that `apps/api/src/lib/ask/tools.ts` is
  the ready-made handler layer whenever the API is built. At 24 signups in 90 days,
  building for a customer's teammates is building for people who do not exist yet.
  Revisit when a paying workspace asks.
- **Another monitoring source.** There are around twenty source types, several of
  them internal anchors. Plan 019's evidence says the binding constraint is that a
  large share of orgs get **no** signal from the sources they already have, which a
  twenty-first source does not fix.
- **A CRM provider OAuth integration** (HubSpot / Salesforce). The generic outbound
  webhook shipped instead, deliberately, per `docs/distribution-team.md:7-11`, and
  `crm_destinations` shows the decision was to avoid external app registration. That
  trade-off holds at this scale.
- **Rebuilding onboarding.** It is instrumented, resumable, has a funnel and a
  session table, and 26 of 26 tracked completions finished. The failure is
  downstream of completion, which is what plan 019 measures.
- **The `apps/web/src/lib/api.ts` refactor and the `useMutation` migration.** Real,
  already scoped by the deep run above, and tech debt rather than direction. Left
  where the deep run put it.
